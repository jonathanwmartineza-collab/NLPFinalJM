# Privacy basics (general information, not legal advice)
Why this matters: products collect and send data; users deserve to know.

- Minimise data; prefer aggregate, cookieless analytics; never log message contents.
- Disclose browser storage (localStorage/IndexedDB) and offer a clear button.
- Name processors (AI provider, hosting, fonts) and what they receive.
- EU/UK: consent needed for non-essential cookies/trackers (ePrivacy/PECR); GDPR principles: lawful basis, purpose limitation, minimisation, retention, rights of access/erasure.
- California (CCPA/CPRA): notice at collection; right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale/sharing.
- AI disclosure: label AI-generated content and advise review before reliance.
- Recommend professional legal review before commercial launch.

Sources: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/ (accessed 2026-11-21); https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa (accessed 2026-11-21)
