# Voice and tone

## Procedure
1. Three voice principles with "this, not that" pairs (e.g. "Clear, not clever").
2. Tone map: for each situation (first use, success, waiting, error, destructive action, legal) give the emotional goal and an example line.
3. Word list: preferred terms, banned jargon, how we name key objects; sentence case everywhere.
4. Style rules: active voice; lead with the outcome; numbers as numerals; plain words at roughly grade 7–9 reading level.
5. Before/after rewrites of 5 real strings from the product.

## Checklist
- [ ] Principles are distinctive and usable by someone else.
- [ ] Error tone is calm and helpful, never blaming.
- [ ] Every example is in sentence case and under ~20 words.
