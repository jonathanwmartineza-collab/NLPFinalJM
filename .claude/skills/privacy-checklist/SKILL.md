# Privacy checklist (general information, not legal advice)

## Procedure
1. Data inventory: what is collected, where it is stored (browser, server, third parties such as the AI provider or hosting), how long, who can access it.
2. Minimise: collect only what the feature needs; prefer aggregate, cookieless analytics; no message content in logs.
3. Browser storage: disclose what stays in localStorage/IndexedDB and give a one-click clear.
4. Third parties: list processors (e.g. Anthropic, Netlify, Google Fonts) and what they receive; link their privacy policies.
5. Voice: browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser vendor's service; say so next to the mic.
6. AI disclosure: label AI-generated content; remind users to review before relying on it; don't present AI output as professional advice.
7. Consent: needed for non-essential cookies/trackers in the EU/UK (GDPR, ePrivacy) and for selling/sharing data under CCPA/CPRA; provide opt-outs where required.
8. Rights: how users request access/deletion; a contact address.
9. Licences: third-party code, fonts, images and skills have compatible licences; keep notices.

## Checklist
- [ ] Privacy note page exists and matches reality.
- [ ] No personal data or content in server logs.
- [ ] AI and voice disclosures are visible where used.
- [ ] Recommend professional legal review before commercial launch.
