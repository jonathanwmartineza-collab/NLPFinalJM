# Competitor teardown

## Procedure
1. Pick 3–5 direct and indirect competitors (including "do nothing" / spreadsheets).
2. For each: target user, promise (headline), pricing model, onboarding steps to first value, core flow, standout UX, weaknesses, accessibility quick check, reviews' top complaints (with sources).
3. Compare in a feature × competitor table; mark parity, gaps, over-served areas.
4. State the opportunity: who is under-served and what we do differently (one sentence).
5. Note what NOT to copy (trademarks, copy, distinctive visuals).

## Checklist
- [ ] Every claim has a source and date; pricing marked with date seen.
- [ ] Time-to-first-value measured in steps.
- [ ] Differentiation is a user benefit, not a feature list.

## Output template
| Competitor | User | Promise | Price | Steps to value | Strength | Weakness |
**Gap to win:** … · **Avoid copying:** …
